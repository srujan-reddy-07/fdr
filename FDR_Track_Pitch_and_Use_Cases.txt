PART 1: 2-MINUTE ELEVATOR PITCH & PROBLEM-SOLUTION SUMMARY
(Use this to explain the project quickly to judges, evaluators, or stakeholders)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. THE PROBLEM (What AICTE faces today):
- As part of the statutory approval and compliance process, AICTE requires technical 
  institutions (engineering, management, pharmacy, technical universities) to submit 
  and maintain security deposits or Fixed Deposit Receipts (FDRs).
- Today, these instruments are managed across disparate offices using fragmented physical 
  registers, manual correspondence, and unlinked spreadsheets.
- Critical consequences:
  a. Lack of single-pane visibility: Officers cannot see the live validity, custody 
     location, or true legal status of deposits.
  b. Missed maturity dates: FDRs expire without timely renewal or conversion, risking 
     unauthorized liquidation.
  c. Manual scrutiny bottlenecks: Cross-checking bank certificates against institution-
     declared numbers and amounts is slow and prone to human oversight.
  d. Audit and security vulnerabilities: Paper trails and spreadsheets can be tampered 
     with, offering no cryptographic provenance.

2. THE SOLUTION (What FDR-Track Delivers):
- FDR-Track is a secure, centralized, role-based lifecycle management portal designed 
  specifically for the AICTE Regulation Bureau.
- Core Innovations:
  a. End-to-End Administrative Lifecycle: Covers initial declaration, document capture, 
     verification, validity/maturity tracking, renewal lineage chains, release/refund 
     requests, and custody tracking.
  b. Three-Tier Trust Labelling: Every data point carries an explicit source badge:
     - INSTITUTION_DECLARED (submitted by college)
     - DOCUMENT_EXTRACTED (extracted via OCR from scanned documents)
     - BANK_CONFIRMED (validated via authorized financial adapters)
  c. Automated Discrepancy & Rules Engine: Instant detection of 11 configurable 
     compliance rules (R01 to R11) such as date order errors, category minimum amounts, 
     duplicate reference numbers, and missing joint/lien wording.
  d. Cryptographic Audit Chain: Append-only ledger where every administrative event is 
     chained using SHA-256 hashes, with a 1-click verification engine that detects any 
     tampering.
  e. Date Simulation Engine: Interactive demo control that advances system time by 
     configurable offsets (e.g., +30 days) to demonstrate real-time escalation and alerts 
     without waiting weeks.
  f. Human-in-the-Loop Governance: Algorithms assist and flag discrepancies, but only 
     authorized human officers make scrutiny decisions and release approvals.

PART 2: THE 10 DETAILED USE CASES & THEIR PRACTICAL USE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

USE CASE 1: Centralized Security Deposit Repository
- Who uses it: Institution Users, Scrutiny Officers, Competent Authority, Auditors, Admins.
- Purpose: Eliminates scattered paper records and unlinked tracking spreadsheets.
- How it works:
  - Consolidates institution-wise and instrument-wise records across the country.
  - Multi-parameter search by FDR reference number, issuing bank, institution name, or state.
  - Instant filtering by administrative workflow status (Submitted, Under Scrutiny, Verified, 
    Release Requested, etc.) and date-driven validity (Active, Expiring Soon, Matured).
  - Institution users see strictly their own institution's portfolio, while bureau officers 
    maintain macro-oversight.

USE CASE 2: Digital Document Ingestion & Verification
- Who uses it: Institution User (upload) & Scrutiny Officer (review).
- Purpose: Secure capture of FDR scans, board resolutions, and bank confirmations.
- How it works:
  - Validates file type, size, and generates a SHA-256 fingerprint upon upload.
  - Links uploaded evidence directly to the corresponding instrument record.
  - Prepares document payloads for OCR text extraction and field comparison.

USE CASE 3: Automated Discrepancy Detection & Scrutiny Assistance
- Who uses it: Scrutiny Officer.
- Purpose: Prevents non-compliant, undersized, or mismatched deposits from being verified.
- Rules Evaluated:
  - R01: Mandatory field missing (reference number, bank name, amount).
  - R02: Invalid date order (maturity date earlier than issue date).
  - R04 / R06: Duplicate FDR reference numbers across institutions.
  - R08: Instrument submitted with an already expired maturity date.
  - R09: AICTE joint-account or lien wording missing (flagged for scrutiny, not fraud).
  - R11: Deposit amount below handbook minimum for the institution's category.
- How it works:
  - Officer clicks "Run Rules". The engine checks the record against configured AICTE 
    thresholds and opens structured Discrepancy items specifying expected vs. found values.
  - Status automatically transitions to DISCREPANCY_OPEN or VERIFIED.

USE CASE 4: Bank Verification Adapter (Simulated Interface)
- Who uses it: Scrutiny Officer & Regional Bureau.
- Purpose: Validates declared instrument numbers with issuing banking networks.
- How it works:
  - Executes a clean adapter interface (BankVerificationService).
  - Upgrades data trust tags from INSTITUTION_DECLARED to BANK_CONFIRMED.
  - Flags simulated mismatches (e.g. test references ending in '99') to demonstrate 
    exception handling.
  - Clearly labelled as "Simulated Adapter" in accordance with SIH principles.

USE CASE 5: Validity & Maturity Tracking with Date Simulation
- Who uses it: Bureau Officers, Compliance Admins, Demo Evaluators.
- Purpose: Proactive monitoring of expiring instruments to prevent unintended lapses.
- How it works:
  - System dynamically computes days to maturity:
    * > 30 days remaining  --> ACTIVE (Sage badge)
    * 0 to 30 days remaining --> EXPIRING_SOON (Amber badge)
    * < 0 days (past maturity) --> MATURED (Brick badge)
  - Features a "+30 Days Simulation" trigger: dynamically advances the comparison clock 
    to demonstrate upcoming expiries, reminders, and urgency shifts in real time.

USE CASE 6: Renewal & Replacement Lineage Monitoring
- Who uses it: Institution Users and Bureau Officers.
- Purpose: Maintains a tamper-proof historical chain when an FDR is rolled over or replaced.
- How it works:
  - When an institution renews an expiring deposit, the new instrument is linked to the 
    preceding deposit via an InstrumentLineage relation.
  - The previous instrument is marked RENEWED_OR_REPLACED while the new one takes over active custody.
  - Provides a complete historical timeline (v1 -> v2 -> v3) preserving all earlier receipts, 
    amounts, and officer notes.

USE CASE 7: Release & Refund Staged Workflow
- Who uses it: Institution User (requests), Scrutiny Officer (recommends), Competent Authority (decides).
- Purpose: Ensures zero security deposits are released without strict compliance clearance.
- How it works:
  - Institution raises a formal release request.
  - Automated conditions checklist runs:
    1. No open discrepancies exist.
    2. Custody status is verified IN_CUSTODY.
    3. Required supporting documents are present.
  - If any condition fails, submission is blocked with explicit diagnostic reasons.
  - Multi-tier approval: Scrutiny Officer reviews and recommends; Competent Authority gives 
    final digital approval or rejection. Custody transitions to RETURNED upon approval.

USE CASE 8: Operational Outbox & Multi-Tier Escalations
- Who uses it: Automated Scheduler, Institution Compliance Officers, Regional Directors.
- Purpose: Eliminates forgotten notifications through automated reminder schedules.
- How it works:
  - Pre-configured reminder offsets at 90, 60, 30, 15, and 7 days prior to maturity.
  - Escalation ladder: sends notifications first to institution officials; if unacknowledged, 
    escalates to regional scrutiny officers and senior authority.
  - Simulated Outbox adapter logs every dispatched notification.

USE CASE 9: Executive Dashboard, KPIs & Impact Reporting
- Who uses it: AICTE Senior Leadership, Bureau Chiefs, Policy Planners.
- Purpose: Strategic oversight of the nationwide security deposit portfolio.
- How it works:
  - Live KPI cards: Total Instruments, Total Synthetic Portfolio Value (INR), Expiring in 
    30 Days, Open Discrepancies, Matured, and Pending Workflow queue.
  - Operational Watchlist highlighting items requiring immediate human intervention.
  - Impact & Measurable Improvement panel: tracks reduction in verification turnaround time, 
    elimination of redundant manual steps, and 100% mandatory field completeness.
  - One-click institutional dataset export in standard CSV format for Excel reporting.

USE CASE 10: Cryptographic Audit Trail & Tamper Verification
- Who uses it: Independent Auditors, Vigilance Officers, System Administrators.
- Purpose: Complete legal accountability, forensic tracking, and non-repudiation.
- How it works:
  - Every create, review, bank verification, discrepancy resolution, and approval event 
    is recorded in an append-only ledger.
  - Hash Chaining: Each log entry stores the SHA-256 hash of its payload plus the hash 
    of the previous log entry.
  - "Verify Audit Chain" Button: Recomputes the cryptographic hash chain from Genesis to 
    the latest transaction. Immediately validates 100% integrity or reports the exact ID 
    of any tampered record.

PART 3: ROLES AND ACCESS CONTROL (RBAC MATRIX)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Institution User:
   - Can submit new FDR declarations.
   - Can view only their own institution's portfolio.
   - Can initiate renewal submissions and request deposit release.
   - Cannot view other institutions or approve releases.

2. Scrutiny Officer (Regional / Bureau):
   - Reviews incoming submissions nationwide or by region.
   - Runs automated rule checks and logs scrutiny observations.
   - Resolves discrepancies with mandatory notes.
   - Recommends release requests for higher authority approval.

3. Competent Authority (Senior Officer):
   - Holds the statutory power to make final release decisions.
   - Reviews release requests recommended by scrutiny officers.
   - Executes digital APPROVE_RELEASE or REJECT_RELEASE.

4. Auditor (Read-Only):
   - Full read-only transparency across all institutions, instruments, and system logs.
   - Inspects the append-only audit trail and verifies cryptographic integrity.
   - Cannot create, modify, or delete any operational records.

5. Administrator:
   - Manages role configurations, demo users, reminder offsets, and baseline rules.
   - Can reset demo data and inspect system diagnostic adapters.
